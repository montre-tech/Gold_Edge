Binance/Bybit prices come from exchange perpetual/spot feeds (USDT-quoted), so levels can differ slightly from MT4/MT5. Biquote instruments use an MT5 broker feed (closer to MT4/MT5), but their volume is tick volume (ticks per bar), the same as MT5 shows for forex/CFDs, and history is limited to roughly 2,000 bars per timeframe.
